Brakes & Air Systems

Brakes are the one thing you don't put off. I service air and hydraulic brake systems on site, so you stay road-legal without hauling the rig to a shop.

On a heavy truck or trailer, a brake or air-system problem is both a safety issue and a compliance issue. I diagnose and repair the whole system, from the pedal to the chambers, and get you back to a rig that stops the way it should.

If your brakes are part of a DOT concern, I can handle the repair and the inspection together so you're not making two trips out of one problem.

Free virtual estimate: describe what it's doing, or send a short video of the leak or the pedal, and I can usually tell you what I'm walking into.

What I handle

  • Air brake service & repair
  • Hydraulic brake service & repair
  • Air-system leaks & pressure loss
  • ABS faults & warning lights
  • Wheel & hub seal replacement
  • Brake work for DOT compliance
Worth a call

Signs to stop and call

Anything on this list is worth catching before your next load.

  • Soft, spongy, or low brake pedal
  • Air leaks or slow pressure build-up
  • Brakes dragging, grabbing, or pulling
  • ABS light on or a warning that won't clear
  • Grinding, squealing, or new brake noise
  • Oil or grease around a wheel or hub
