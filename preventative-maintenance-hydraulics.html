Preventative Maintenance & Hydraulics

The cheapest repair is the one you never have to make. I keep your trucks and equipment on a service schedule, and when a hydraulic system goes down, I fix it in the field.

Staying ahead of the wear is what keeps a rig earning instead of sitting. I handle scheduled service, fluids and filters, and the wear items that strand you when they're ignored, all on your yard or job site on a schedule that works around your runs.

Hydraulics are their own animal. A blown line, a weak pump, or a leaking cylinder can stop a machine cold. I diagnose the system under load, find the leak or the pressure loss, and rebuild or replace what's failed.

Free virtual estimate: tell me your hours or your service interval, or send a video of the leak, and I'll tell you what it needs.

Worth a call

Signs to stop and call

Catch these early and you keep a service call from turning into a breakdown.

  • Slow, weak, or jerky hydraulic movement
  • Oil on the ground under a machine
  • A service interval that's overdue
  • Whining or foaming from the hydraulic system
  • A cylinder that drifts or won't hold
  • Fluid that's dark, milky, or low
