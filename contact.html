Get in touch

Call Or Text. I'll Pick Up.

You call, you get me, not a service writer or a call center. Tell me what you're running and what it's doing, and I'll tell you straight what it'll take.

The fastest way to reach me is a call or a text. If I'm under a truck when you ring, I'll get back to you as soon as I'm out from under it, usually within minutes.

When you reach out, it helps to have three things ready: what you run (year, make, and model if you have it), what it's doing, and where it's parked. With that, I can usually tell you over the phone whether it's a quick fix or a bigger job, and get you a plan either way.

Free virtual estimate: send a photo or a short video of the problem by text and I'll give you a real read on it before I ever roll out, no charge and no obligation.

Before you call

What to have ready

None of this is required. It just gets you a faster, straighter answer on the first call.

  • What you run: year, make, and model if you have it
  • What it's doing: the noise, the leak, the fault code, the warning light
  • Where it's parked: yard, job site, or the side of the road
  • Whether it still runs and drives or it's down cold
  • A photo or short video by text, if you can grab one
  • How soon you need it back in service
