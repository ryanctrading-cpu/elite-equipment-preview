Diesel & Heavy Engine Repair

When a diesel engine is down, hauling it in is the expensive part. I bring the diagnostics and the repair to your yard, the job site, or the shoulder of the road.

I work on heavy-duty diesel and domestic engines in Class 8 trucks, motorhomes, and farm and construction equipment. Most calls start the same way: something changed, and you need to know what it is and what it'll take before you commit to anything.

I plug in, read the codes, and track the fault down to the actual cause instead of throwing parts at it. Then I tell you straight what it needs, what it costs, and what can wait. If you want to understand the repair while I'm doing it, I'll walk you through it.

Free virtual estimate: send a few photos or a quick video and a description, and I can usually give you a read before I roll out.

  • Hard starts & no-start issues
  • Power loss & drivability

Worth a call

Signs to stop and call

Catching these early is usually the difference between a repair and a breakdown.

  • Check-engine or warning lights that won't clear
  • Noticeable loss of power or poor fuel economy
  • Knocking, ticking, or new engine noise
  • White, black, or blue smoke from the stack
  • Oil or coolant on the ground where you park
  • Hard starts, long cranks, or a no-start
