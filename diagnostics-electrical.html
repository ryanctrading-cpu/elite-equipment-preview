Diagnostics & Electrical

A warning light or a no-start doesn't have to mean a tow. I bring the scan tools to you, find the actual fault, and fix the electrical problem where your rig sits.

Modern diesels and heavy equipment throw codes for everything, and a code is a starting point, not an answer. I plug in, read what the truck is actually telling me, and trace the fault back to the real cause instead of swapping parts and hoping.

Most of what strands a rig is electrical: a dead battery bank, a charging system that isn't keeping up, a starter that won't engage, or a sensor feeding bad data. I test the whole circuit, from the battery to the component, and tell you straight what it needs.

Free virtual estimate: send the code, a photo of the dash, or a quick description, and I can usually tell you what I'm walking into before I roll out.

Worth a call

Signs to stop and call

Electrical problems rarely fix themselves. Catching one early keeps it from stranding you.

  • Warning or check-engine light that won't clear
  • Slow crank, clicking, or a no-start
  • Dash gauges reading wrong or dropping out
  • Battery going dead overnight
  • Dimming lights or a charging warning
  • Corroded, melted, or chafed wiring
