Where I work

The Whole Tri-Cities, And Then Some

I run out of Pasco and cover Kennewick and Richland every week, plus the towns around them and the wider Pacific Northwest. Wherever your rig sits, that's where I work on it.

Being mobile is the whole point. You don't limp a down truck across town or pay to haul a dead machine to a shop. I load the tools and come to your yard, your job site, or the shoulder of the highway.

Home base is the Tri-Cities, so Pasco, Kennewick, and Richland get the fastest turnaround. From there I cover the surrounding towns and, for bigger jobs and fleet work, I'll travel across Washington, Oregon, and Idaho. If you're not sure whether you're in range, the answer is usually yes, so just call and ask.

Free virtual estimate: text me a photo or video of the problem and I'll give you a read before I ever roll out.
