On-site service plans

Scheduled Maintenance, Tracked By Unit

The cheapest repair is the one that never has to happen. I keep your trucks and equipment on a service schedule, track every interval, and handle the work where your equipment sits.

Downtime costs more than the service does. I keep a record for every unit you run: serial number, make, model, and current hours or miles, along with what's already been done. So when a machine comes due, I already know its history and exactly what the manufacturer calls for at that interval.

Your operators handle the daily 10 and 50-hour checks. I handle the real service intervals, from the 250-hour service up through the major 1,000, 2,000, and manufacturer-specific work, on your yard or job site on a schedule built around your runs. If a service turns up something worn, I can repair it on the same visit.

Free virtual estimate: tell me what you run and how hard you run it, and I'll put together a maintenance plan that fits.
