Who you're calling

The Mechanic Who Explains It

Elite Equipment is a one-man mobile operation. When you call, you get me, not a service writer, and I'll tell you straight what your rig needs.

I've been wrenching for over ten years, the last five professionally, with a background as a dealership and construction-company mechanic. I genuinely like taking something that isn't running and making it work again.

Because I'm mobile, there's no hauling the vehicle anywhere. I bring the shop to you, on your yard, at the job site, or on the side of the road, anywhere in the Pacific Northwest. You get honest, reliable, efficient work.

If you're the kind of owner who wants to understand the repair, I'll walk you through it start to finish. No mystery, no runaround. Just a clear answer on what it takes and what it costs.
