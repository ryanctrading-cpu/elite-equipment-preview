On-site inspections

Inspections, Done On Site

Whether you need to stay DOT-legal, keep equipment ANSI-compliant, or know what you're buying before you sign, I do the inspection where your rig or machine sits.

A DOT inspection isn't the place to cut corners. I go through the truck or trailer the way an inspector would, catch what would fail you, and can handle the repair on the spot so you're not making two trips out of one problem.

For heavy equipment, ANSI inspections keep your machines compliant and your crew safe. And if you're about to buy a used truck, trailer, or piece of equipment, a pre-purchase inspection tells you what it's really worth before the money changes hands.

Free virtual estimate: tell me the unit and what you need signed off, and I'll tell you what the inspection covers and what it runs.

  • ANSI equipment inspections
  • Pre-purchase inspections
  • Brake & air system checks
  • Lights, tires & undercarriage
  • Repairs to pass, done on site
